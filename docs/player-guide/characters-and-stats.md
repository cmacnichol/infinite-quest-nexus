# Characters and statistics

The campaign's player character is selected when the campaign is created. Nexus copies that character into the campaign so later world-draft edits do not change an ongoing story.

## View the campaign character

Open **Setup** in the universal navigation and select **Current World Setup**. The read-only view includes:

- Character name and guidance
- RPG statistics
- Current trackers
- Pinned world and version
- Genre and tone
- Premise, background, and rules

## Mechanics privacy

The Story Engine can privately assess an action and resolve a typed mechanic. The player may see a concise roll outcome, but random values, targets, trigger reasons, diagnostics, and scratchpads remain outside narration, Chronicle memory, embeddings, and illustration prompts.

Retries reuse persisted private resolution where required; they do not reroll merely because narration needs recovery.

## Change the reusable character

World authors can revise characters in an editable world draft and publish a new version. That does not mutate existing campaign character snapshots. Moving a campaign to a new world version is an explicit Nexus management operation.


## Campaign Characters

Open **Characters** from Story to browse the main character and supporting cast. Search by name or alias, or narrow the list to recent, pinned, ignored, or review-needed characters. Select a character to read its sheet and expand source evidence. Editing the main character uses labeled profile fields and preserves information not shown in the form; supporting-character corrections remain separate from the protagonist profile.

Tracking runs after accepted story turns when enabled. A text mention is not proof that someone is present. Automatic name matches retain their sources; choose **Not this character** on the character sheet to undo a mistaken match. Undo removes only the linked evidence, preserves your corrections, and returns the identity to review when its source is still available.

Use the review inbox to resolve identities and to confirm or dismiss individual withheld details. Confirmation records your correction; dismissal leaves the character unchanged. Older history is scanned only when you explicitly preview and start a range. Tracking status distinguishes disabled, catching up, complete, and failed work.

Use **Likely included for this input** to estimate which characters fit the current story context. Enter the intended story input and refresh the estimate. It explains selection and omissions within the available budget without sending a model request. Refresh after the story or characters change; later routing and action assessment can change the final selection.
