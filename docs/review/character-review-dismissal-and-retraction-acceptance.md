# Character review dismissal and retraction acceptance

## Stage A: verified; Stage B: pending

Stage A covers source-scoped pending identity and detail dismissal, separate decisions, and Undo. Skip remains temporary. A dismissal does not remove a turn or accepted fact and is not a global ban on a name. A corrected narration or evidence from another turn can return to review. Journal dismissals are excluded from branches and archives; older pre-journal dismissals remain effective but cannot be listed or undone. Character removal remains governed by the existing lifecycle capability, and the interface explains when it is disabled. Stage B accepted-fact retraction and its portability requirements are pending; this record does not approve those later writers.

The composed proof caught a continuity defect: creating a character after dismissing a detail could allow a rescan that supplied the new character ID to publish that detail. The reviewed fix binds the retained source and proposal to a unique resolved identity, retains distinct-person isolation, and falls back conservatively when bindings are ambiguous. PostgreSQL tests cover create and attach, automatic world seeding, distinct people sharing a quotation, ambiguous persisted identity bindings, changed sources and stale Undo. No schema or prompt change was needed for this fix.
