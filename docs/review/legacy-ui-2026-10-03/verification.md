## Evidence rules

- Unit, pure-policy, mocked-browser, real-PostgreSQL, and runtime evidence are different classes. A mock server or fake database port does not prove PostgreSQL or production behavior.
- Historical task screenshots linked below are local, ignored artifacts. The final visual matrix uses portable PNGs under docs/review/assets/legacy-ui-2026-10-03/T35-final with capture manifests. Each capture retains its synthetic, mocked, or disposable-runtime evidence class; it is not a live-provider or production screenshot.
- Historical failures, fixture failures, superseded runs, and intentional red cases remain failures or diagnostics; they are not converted into passes by a later task summary. The implementation log records material corrections and exclusions.
- The T01 benchmark is a mocked development-server observation. T28’s diagnostic is a Windows/Vite mocked comparison, not matched C0 or production latency. The final matched observations are recorded separately in performance.md; no production performance conclusion follows from these earlier measurements.
- The recent 135-unit and 19-browser result belongs to the T29 checkpoint only. The 19 browser passes comprise 12 T29 cases and 7 T27 cases; they do not cover every accepted task.
- Historical screenshot links into ignored `.superpowers/` evidence work only in the evidence-owning checkout. Final portable screenshots are committed separately; raw logs and private runtime configuration stay local.
